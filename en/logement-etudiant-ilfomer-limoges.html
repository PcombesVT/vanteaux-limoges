ILFOMER, the rehabilitation professions institute of Limoges

Physiotherapy, OT, speech and psychomotor therapy: ILFOMER brings together the main rehabilitation programmes in Limoges, on the Vanteaux campus — with students who often come from elsewhere and look for housing at the start of the year.

ILFOMER (the Limousin Institute for Training in Rehabilitation Professions), part of the University of Limoges, trains in the paramedical rehabilitation professions: physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy and psychomotor therapy. These 3-to-5-year programmes combine theory, practical work and clinical placements, notably at the CHU teaching hospital of Limoges and partner health facilities.

Located on the Vanteaux campus, south-west of Limoges, the institute shares its area with the FLSH and ENSAD, and is a short distance from the Marcland health campus and the CHU Dupuytren — a key location when you alternate classes and placements.

Living near ILFOMER: the Vanteaux district

For a student in physiotherapy, OT, speech or psychomotor therapy, living in Vanteaux means reaching the institute on foot — and being closer to the CHU for placements, without wasting time commuting.

The Vanteaux district, south-west of Limoges, is one of the most coherent areas for students of the campus and the health hub. It combines residential calm, green spaces, local shops and good bus links to the city centre and the Bénédictins station. It is also one of the city's best-value areas — Limoges remaining one of the most affordable university cities in France.

Student housing in Limoges, in practice. A student studio usually rents from around €280 to €400/month including charges depending on size and fittings, and remains eligible for CAF housing benefit (APL / ALS), which clearly reduces the net cost. Demand is high at the start of the year: it's best to start your search in the previous spring.

Begip, a local agency established in Limoges for over 35 years, manages a wide portfolio of homes in the Vanteaux area and nearby: furnished or unfurnished studios and small flats, suited to the pace and budget of a rehabilitation student. The team supports every tenant from viewing to key handover, and guides you through the CAF paperwork.
