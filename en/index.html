The district's history

From rural estate to residential district

Vanteaux was not born of an urban plan. Its story unfolds over several centuries, from the rural land south of Limoges to today's university hub.

Long a rural estate on the edge of Limoges, Vanteaux originally referred to a farmed, wooded area on the heights of the city's south-west. The place name points to an old etymology, possibly linked to the idea of a valley or a draught of air, like other hamlets across the Limousin.

Through the 20th century, the growth of Limoges gradually absorbed these outlying areas. Vanteaux became a residential district, shaped by post-war housing schemes and by a determination to preserve the leafy character of the area. Detached houses, well-kept small apartment blocks and wide tree-lined roads: development here was measured.

The arrival of the University of Limoges — through the Vanteaux campus and the gradual creation of the Faculty of Arts and Humanities (FLSH), then the National School of Art and Design (ENSA) — transformed the district. Vanteaux became a higher-education hub, drawing hundreds of students each year and bringing together generations, backgrounds and paths.

The immediate proximity of Dupuytren teaching hospital, opened in the second half of the 20th century, gave Vanteaux its present-day identity: a district balanced between residential tradition, university energy and a regional medical role.

Today, Vanteaux is one of the sought-after areas of Limoges: it offers the rarity of a green, quiet environment right next to major activity hubs, and just ten minutes from the historic city centre.

FAQ

Vanteaux, answered

The most common questions about the district, its transport, its property market and its atmosphere.

Vanteaux lies in south-west Limoges, in postcode 87000. It's about 10 to 15 minutes by bus from the historic city centre. The district is bounded roughly by Boulevard de Vanteaux, Rue François Perrin and the area around Dupuytren teaching hospital. It adjoins the town of Isle.

Vanteaux is one of the most popular residential districts in Limoges. It combines several strengths: a green setting, a quiet environment, decent STCL bus services, the immediate proximity of the university campus and the teaching hospital, and a varied housing stock (from studio to family house). It's sought after by students, young professionals and families alike.

The Vanteaux campus is home to several major institutions: the Faculty of Arts and Humanities (FLSH), the National School of Art and Design (ENSA), the IFSI (nursing) and the ILFOMER (physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy). The district also has several nursery and primary schools, plus secondary schools nearby. See the full list →

Several STCL bus lines serve Vanteaux from Limoges city centre: mainly lines 10, 12, 14 and D102. The trip takes between 10 and 20 minutes depending on the line and stop. By car, allow about 10 minutes off-peak via Boulevard Gambetta then Avenue Garibaldi.

Rents in Vanteaux vary with the type and condition of the property. As a guide: a furnished studio in a student residence starts around €340–400/month including charges; a one-bedroom flat (T2) in the private market is generally between €450 and €600/month; family houses and larger flats (T3/T4) are above that. Homes may qualify for CAF housing benefit (APL or ALS) depending on their status. See the housing options →

Yes, the Vanteaux district and its immediate surroundings have all the everyday shops: bakeries, pharmacies, supermarkets (Super U Perrin, Carrefour City), a newsagent-tobacconist, restaurants and bars. Several medical practices and healthcare professionals are also based here. See all the shops →

Vanteaux is generally seen as a peaceful, residential district. Its overwhelmingly detached-housing character, its mixed population (long-established families, students, hospital staff) and its green setting make it a quiet part of Limoges. As with any urban area, the usual sensible precautions apply.
